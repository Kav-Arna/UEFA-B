## Theme

The same read as First Touch, Find the Target — he jumps, pass; he holds, drive — but now a genuine cover defender exists, so the "pass" resolution has to go through a support player rather than direct to a target. This brings Space 2 and Space 3 into live play.

## Setup

- **Numbers:** 3v3, plus a neutral target (3v3+1).
- **Grid:** roughly 25m x 18m, small mini-goals or an end zone to attack.
- **Constraint:** the two attackers not on the ball must manipulate the third defender — occupying or dragging him — before the 2v1 combination can be scored. A goal only counts if the third defender was moved out of a central covering position first.

## Scoring

Goals count double if the third defender was visibly dragged out of position by off-ball movement before the ball arrived. This is the scoring-multiplier mechanic from the Thin/Thick/Full method: it doesn't change what the game is, it changes what's worth noticing inside it.

## Coaching Points

- **Space 1:** the on-ball duel — same read as the thin slice.
- **Space 2:** the immediate support player creating the passing lane off the fixed defender — underlap, overlap, or third-man run, not standing in a flat line.
- **Space 3:** the player away from the ball — "are you making yourself a problem for their spare defender, or are you standing still?"

## Progression

Add a second cover defender and widen the grid slightly — this is the natural bridge into **Free the Second Man**, the full-slice scrimmage version of the same theme.
