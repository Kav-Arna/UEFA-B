## Theme

By this point the read (pin-or-bait, Space 1) and the support movement (Space 2/3) have both been isolated and combined. The full slice asks: does it show up unprompted in something close to a real game, under a light nudge rather than a hard constraint?

## Setup

- **Numbers:** 5v5, plus two floating neutrals (5v5+2) who play for whichever team is in possession.
- **Grid:** roughly 40m x 30m, full-size mini-goals at each end.
- **Constraint:** none beyond normal game rules — this is the point of the full slice. The theme is carried by scoring, not by added rules.

## Scoring

A goal scored from a 2v1 combination that started with a player dribbling directly at a defender — pin or bait, then a support pass or a drive into conceded space — is worth double. Everything else counts as normal. No whistle is needed to identify it; it's visible enough for both teams to know when it's happened.

## Coaching Points

- Resist the urge to freeze the game and explain — the whole point of the full slice is that the read either shows up under real pressure or it doesn't. Note it for the debrief instead.
- If the double-goal mechanic isn't being chased at all, that's information: drop back to Drag the Cover for a session rather than trying to force it here.
- This is also where the On-Pitch Prompts cue — "he jumps, pass; he holds, drive" — should be usable as a live in-game prompt, not just a training-ground instruction.

## Where This Sits in the Season

Per the Game Model season arc, this full-slice version is the kind of session that can also double as (or feed) the matchday warm-up's representative activity once the theme has been embedded through the earlier two slices.
