## Theme

One theme, per the Thin/Thick/Full method: a player on the ball reads whether his direct opponent jumps or holds, and resolves it correctly — pass if he jumps, drive if he holds. This is Space 1 in isolation, tied to a single Step 2 confrontation.

## Setup

- **Numbers:** 2v2, plus a neutral target player (2v2+1).
- **Grid:** roughly 15m x 12m, no goals — the neutral target stands on the end line and scores the practice by receiving a pass played through a defender who jumped.
- **Constraint:** the on-ball player must dribble at his direct opponent before any pass is played — no first-time balls around him. This forces the pin-or-bait duel to happen every time, rather than being avoidable.

## Scoring (diagnostic, not competitive)

A team scores by finding the neutral target with a pass that was only available *because* the defender jumped. Driving into the space when the defender holds also counts as a successful read, even without a score — the coaching point is the read, not just the end product.

## Coaching Points

- **Space 1:** "What did he do — jump or hold? What does that tell you?"
- First touch out of pressure has to set up the read, not just escape it — a heavy touch away from the defender removes the choice before it's made.
- No punishing a "wrong" resolution if the read was accurate — a correctly-read hold that gets dispossessed by a recovering defender is still a successful read.

## Progression

Remove the "must dribble first" constraint once the read is happening reliably — this is the bridge into **Drag the Cover**, where a third attacker enters and Space 2/3 responsibilities come into the picture.
